## Known limitations (detector — not RC gate regressions)

- **Payment card numbers in free text:** built-in **`CREDIT_CARD`** detection targets **16-digit** PAN shapes (4-4-4-4, including TAB/NBSP separators per **#1978**). **Luhn-valid Amex (15-digit) and Diners (14-digit)** strings in unstructured content are **not** matched by that regex today; when the **PCI sample override** (`PCI_CARD_COLUMN` in `docs/compliance-samples/compliance-sample-pci_dss.yaml`) is configured, **named** card columns may still be flagged. Remediation tracked for **v1.8.1** (**data-boar#1984**), not a blocker for this RC tag.
